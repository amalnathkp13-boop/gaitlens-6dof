What changes in the build

  1. Replace the joint-constraint "STA toggle" with flexion–varus crosstalk minimisation. Shrinking secondary rotations toward a rigid-joint model removes exactly the varus thrust clinicians look for. Crosstalk minimisation recovers 94% of true thrust and separates OA from healthy knees perfectly in this cohort. On stage, toggle drift and crosstalk correction instead.
  2. Do the functional calibration in two passes. Screw-home rotation tilts a gyro-fitted hinge axis by 10–15°. Refit using samples above 35° flexion, take the shank axis from the standing knee-bend trial, and align the two sensors' headings from flexed postures, not from standing. Skipping this nearly triples varus error.
  3. Report translations as model estimates. Neither the IMUs nor a pose-estimation camera observe millimetre tibiofemoral translation. ACL-deficient knees were indistinguishable from healthy ones (AUC 0.44), and fitting translation from camera landmarks made it worse. Present 3 measured rotations + 3 model-derived translations with stated uncertainty.
  4. Drop the camera from the accuracy story, keep it for the jury. Standing pauses and stride stationarity already hold drift flat; the camera adds about 0.1°. Use it for the side-by-side video, flexion cross-check and time sync (3.5 ms), and say so.
  5. Add a foot or shank event offset, or the 6th foot sensor. Shank-gyro heel strikes run 38 ms late. A fixed offset learned on a pressure mat brings it to 12 ms.
  6. Swap the ankle-weight demo for a lateral-wedge or valgus-brace demo. An ankle weight mostly changes swing, so it is a weak stand-in for varus loading. A 5–10° wedge or unloader brace changes frontal-plane kinematics directly. This one is engineering judgement, not simulated.

Not simulated: KAM (needs kinetics and a force-plate-trained model), BLE and phone latency (compute per window is under 0.1 ms), battery life. Truth curves are normative-shaped synthetic kinematics, not cadaver or fluoroscopy data, so absolute numbers should be re-checked on a real jig before quoting them.
